Studio.

Visuospatials is a place for design and artwork made with other people, published without asking anyone's permission.

01

The idea

Most work gets made alone and then shown alone. This is the other arrangement: two or more people, one surface, and no house style to protect.

Sound, image, print, screen. The medium is whatever the piece turns out to need.

02

How it runs

A piece starts as a conversation and ends as something you can look at. No brief, no deck, no committee. If it works it goes up here; if it does not it stays in the drawer, which is also fine.

Nothing here is behind a login. Everything published stays published.

03

The tools

The lab holds the instruments — halftone, moiré, kinetic type, a poster machine. They run in your browser and export at print resolution. Take what you make; no credit needed.

Bring something

Send a link, a rough, a half-finished thing. Loose ideas are welcome; finished pitches less so.
